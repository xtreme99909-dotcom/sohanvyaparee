Private market is a speculative interface study. Clear proof earns more trust than inflated claims.

## 10. Inbound DM qualification

DMs are for inbound interest and separately approved follow-up only. Do not cold-pitch, attach a free redesign, send a generic audit, issue a proposal, send a meeting link, quote a price or duplicate contact in another channel.

### First response

> Thanks for reaching out. What changed in the business, and what should the website help the right customer do?

### Authority check

> Are you the founder or the person responsible for the final website decision?

### Scope and readiness check

> Please send the current owned website journey, main functionality, what content/access is ready and the target launch period.

### Investment check

Only after the earlier gates are credible:

> Is there an approved investment range for the project, and which currency does the business need the proposal in?

Do not respond with four competing packages.

### Scopeable response

> This appears scopeable. The next step is the structured project brief so I can confirm one recommended scope, fixed amount, currency and delivery window: [authoritative URL].

A meeting is offered only after the brief supports it.

### Not-fit response

> Thanks for the detail. This is not the right SP Studios fit because [specific reason]. I do not want to force the wrong service. The better next route is [narrow factual alternative].

A view, reaction or friendly message is not qualification. Any follow-up requires headquarters approval and must not duplicate another worker’s channel.
