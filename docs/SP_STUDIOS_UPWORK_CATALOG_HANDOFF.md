## Requirements

Mandatory:

1. **Need/trigger:** What changed now, and why is the website needed?
2. **Authority:** Who is the single final approval owner? Give the role only; no private contact details.
3. **Outcome:** What should a qualified visitor do, understand and trust?
4. **Scope:** List every page, feature, form, embed, integration and collection; confirm the tier covers all.
5. **Content/proof:** Supply approved business facts, services, products, team information, authorized proof/testimonials and client-supplied legal copy.
6. **Assets:** Supply available logo, colors, fonts, photography and guidance, or state none exist.
7. **Timing:** State the real deadline, business reason and blocking dependencies.
8. **Boundary:** Confirm excluded systems are not expected unless written into funded Upwork scope.

Conditional Advanced requirement:

> Name the tool and describe the single trigger, information transferred and expected result. Do not provide a password, key or OTP.

Optional:

> Share the existing site and up to three references, explaining the useful quality in each. Communication remains on Upwork.

Use secure access invitations after contract start. Never request passwords, OTPs, CVV, bank credentials, private phone numbers or private email addresses.

## FAQ

**Can I purchase immediately?** Only if the selected tier exactly covers pages, features, platform and timing. Otherwise message through Upwork first.

**Why are profile prices starting contexts?** The profile covers broader qualified engagements. A Catalog tier is narrower and fixed only for its written boundary.

**What is a revision?** One consolidated set of refinements against the agreed direction. New direction, pages, features, integration or strategy are new scope.

**Is copy included?** Message hierarchy and refinement of verified supplied facts are included within limits. Research-heavy original copy is an add-on.

**Hosting/domain?** Deployment and connection are included; the client owns and pays provider costs.

**SEO?** A technical and on-page foundation is included. Rankings, traffic, leads, sales and revenue are not guaranteed.

**Store/payments?** Not included. Commerce, accounts, databases and dashboards require separate qualification.

**One integration?** One confirmed workflow with one third-party service, one trigger and one result. Repairing the service is excluded.

**AI?** It may support research, exploration, development and testing. Sohan directs message, original design and quality. No invented proof.

**Communication/payment?** Pre-contract communication stays on Upwork. Work starts only against funded Upwork project funds/escrow; scope, files and submission stay in Upwork.
