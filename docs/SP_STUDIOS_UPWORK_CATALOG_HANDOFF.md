## Resolved operating decision

**Use a public, tightly bounded fixed-tier Project Catalog. Use qualification-first only for work outside those fixed boundaries.**

This is required by Upwork's mechanics:

1. A buyer selects a tier, checks out, funds it and only then submits requirements. There is no freelancer approval gate before purchase.
2. Mandatory requirements are onboarding inputs, not a pre-sale qualification step. The project clock starts when they are submitted.
3. The buyer has 48 hours to submit mandatory requirements or Upwork automatically cancels and refunds the order.
4. A freelancer may cancel within 24 hours of receiving requirements without Upwork consequences, but cancellation is a recovery mechanism, not a premium sales path.
5. A visible Catalog project is purchasable. Profile privacy does not prevent purchases.
6. Workload can be controlled by turning Catalog visibility off or setting the simultaneous-order limit. The default may be 20, so this offer must explicitly use **1 concurrent order**.

Therefore:

- The USD 1,500 / 3,000 / 5,000 Catalog amounts are complete fixed prices for only the exact scopes below.
- The broader profile ladder remains starting context after qualification.
- Any uncertain, custom, commerce, portal, multilingual, migration-heavy or integration-led project must not be purchased from Catalog. It routes through Upwork Messages into the existing qualification and one-scope proposal lane.
- Requirements confirm and start an already purchased fixed scope; they must not be treated as permission to re-quote the order.
- Cancellation is reserved for genuine mismatch or capacity failure.
- Catalog visibility should be switched off before editing the existing live offer and remain off until final preview QA and explicit headquarters approval.

## Exact FAQ

### Can I purchase this project immediately?

Yes, only when the selected package exactly covers every page, feature, platform and timing need. If any requirement is uncertain or excluded, message me through Upwork before purchasing so a custom fixed scope can be prepared.

### Why are profile prices described as starting contexts?

The profile covers broader qualified engagements. A Project Catalog package is narrower: its displayed price is fixed only for the exact written boundary.

### What counts as one page?

One unique routed layout, such as Home, About, Services or Contact. Product records, blog posts, dashboards, portals, member areas and bulk content entries are not ordinary pages.

### What counts as a revision?

One revision is one consolidated set of refinements against the agreed direction. A new direction, page, feature, integration or strategy is new scope.

### Is website copy included?

Each package includes message hierarchy and refinement of verified supplied facts within its word limit. Drafting new copy from client source material is available through the copy add-on. Research-heavy or regulated copy requires custom scope.

### Are domain and hosting included?

Deployment and domain connection are included. The client owns and pays for the domain, hosting, licenses, assets and third-party subscriptions.

### Is SEO included?

The website includes a technical and on-page foundation: page titles, descriptions, semantic structure and sitemap. Rankings, traffic, leads, sales and revenue are not guaranteed.

### Does Advanced include a store or payment system?

No. Commerce, checkout, payments, accounts, databases and dashboards are excluded from all Catalog packages.

### What does one standard integration mean?

Advanced includes one client-controlled calendar embed, provider-native newsletter/CRM form, or single-direction form webhook to one destination. Custom authentication, two-way sync, migration, multi-step automation and custom APIs are excluded.

### Which platform do you use?

These fixed packages use Next.js and TypeScript. If another CMS or commerce platform is required, message through Upwork before purchase for custom qualification.

### How is AI used?

AI-assisted tools may support research, exploration, development and testing. Sohan personally directs the business message, original design and final quality. No invented proof or result is permitted.

### How are communication and payment handled?

All pre-contract communication stays in Upwork. Work starts only after the Catalog order is funded and mandatory requirements are submitted. Scope, files, revisions and final submission remain in the Upwork workroom and payment flow.
