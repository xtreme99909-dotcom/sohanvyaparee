## 5. Inbound DM response path

DMs are inbound qualification only. No cold outreach, free redesign, generic audit, attachment, proposal, meeting link, price, multi-channel follow-up or automatic response is authorized.

### Trigger A — “What do you do?”

> SP Studios directs complete business websites for founders—from direction and original design through development, integrations and launch. The clearest overview and work proof are here: https://thespstudios.com/work

Stop there unless the person states a current business need.

### Trigger B — Current website need

First response:

> Thanks for reaching out. What changed in the business, and what should the website help the right customer do?

This checks need and outcome. Do not ask six questions at once.

### Gate 2 — Authority

After a credible need:

> Are you the founder or the person responsible for the final website decision?

### Gate 3 — Scope, readiness and timing

> Please send the current owned website journey, the main functionality you expect, what content or access is ready and the target launch period.

Do not offer a free audit. Record only the information the person voluntarily supplies.

### Gate 4 — Investment

Only when need, authority, outcome, scope shape, readiness and timing are credible:

> Is there an approved investment range for the project, and which currency does the business need the proposal in?

Do not reply with the four offer tiers. One buyer receives one later recommendation.

### Scopeable handoff

> This appears scopeable. Please send the structured project brief so I can confirm one recommended scope, fixed amount, currency and realistic delivery window: https://thespstudios.com/#contact

Do not send a meeting link before the brief supports a call.

### Needs one clarification

> Before I route you to the brief, I need one detail: [single missing qualification fact].

Ask only the smallest missing question.

### Not fit — no real trigger

> Thanks for explaining it. I cannot identify a current website trigger or ownership gap, so I would not recommend starting an SP Studios engagement yet.

### Not fit — coding-only task

> This sounds like an isolated implementation task rather than the complete website engagements SP Studios leads. I do not want to force the wrong service.

### Not fit — budget mismatch

> The approved investment does not fit the complete scope described. I would rather be clear than compress the work into an unreliable version.

Do not automatically offer the USD 1,500 Focused Launch. It is appropriate only when the need itself qualifies as narrow and ready.

### Not fit — authority missing

> I can continue once the final website decision-maker is included and the business outcome is clear.

### Follow-up boundary

No follow-up is authorized by this pack. If headquarters later approves one, use the same channel, one owner and one specific missing next step. A view, reaction, friendly reply or brief start is not qualification.
