## Objective

Build and execute a focused client-acquisition and delivery system for Sohan Vyaparee that attracts businesses needing complete, market-ready websites from strategy and original design through development, integrations and launch.

The desired operating model is:

1. the system researches, qualifies, drafts, builds, checks, tracks and prepares routine work;
2. Sohan directs the visual/business standard, approves consequential actions and performs final testing;
3. external activity remains evidence-based, personal and controlled;
4. a qualified buyer moves through one reliable path from need to captured milestone and delivery; and
5. verified client outcomes improve proof and future demand.

## Positioning

SP Studios is a founder-led website strategy, design and development studio. It is not positioned as:

- a job seeker;
- a coding-only freelancer;
- a CGI or animation service;
- a cheap-template or mass-outreach seller;
- an agency claiming invented staff, clients or results; or
- a payment intermediary for third-party work.

The clearest promise is: **complete business websites—from direction and original design through development, integrations and launch.**

Public language must be plain enough for a busy founder to understand in one glance. Premium means clarity, judgement, original design, reliable implementation and controlled delivery—not abstract wording.

## Ideal client

Prioritize a real business with:

- a launch, expansion, international-growth, customer-journey or operational trigger;
- a weak, fragmented, social-only, aggregator-led or absent owned website journey;
- a reachable decision-maker or credible routing contact;
- content, access and approval readiness or a realistic plan to obtain them;
- a primary customer action that a website can materially improve; and
- investment fit for a complete market-ready engagement.

Avoid businesses with a strong owned website, no current trigger, no credible operating evidence, or an incumbent partner with no identifiable ownership gap.

## Qualification and commercial path

The path is:

**interest → need → authority → outcome → scope → readiness → timing → investment → fixed proposal → completed SOW/agreement → verified captured milestone → onboarding → project start**

A lead is scopeable only when need, authority, outcome, scope shape, timing and investment fit are credible.

First contact should show one specific business observation and ask permission for the smallest useful next step. It must not include a free redesign, generic audit, attachment, proposal, meeting link, price or multi-channel follow-up.

Never duplicate contact across Instagram, LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp, phone or forms. A reaction, connection acceptance, message view or friendly conversation is not qualification.

## Current proof boundary

Every public example must be labelled honestly as one of:

- live founder-built work;
- a self-initiated business system; or
- a speculative concept.

Never invent conversion improvement, sales, users, client approval, business impact or a paid relationship. Strong proof explains the business problem, Sohan's direction, included design/build/integration scope, decisions, interactions, test evidence and current limitation.

The live site includes the real founder identity and a pixel-art SP Buddy. The mascot supports memory and interaction; it must not replace clear offer, proof or founder trust.

## Payment boundary

The repository contains a locally verified P0 payment-safety checkpoint. It is not proof that live SP Studios payments are activated.

Preserve:

- branded payment review before provider checkout;
- stable/idempotent payment-link issuance;
- durable local reservation before provider creation;
- provider notifications/reminders disabled;
- provider account, reference, amount, currency and captured-state verification;
- retryable webhook processing with explicit ledger states;
- mismatch quarantine; and
- no collection or storage of OTP, CVV, card or bank credentials.

Live activation still requires verified merchant/KYC and settlement-bank state, correct account/category, international approval when relevant, server-only secrets, webhook configuration, test evidence, CA-confirmed invoice/export treatment and counsel-reviewed agreement/refund/dispute terms.

No worker may activate payments, create keys, enter credentials, change KYC/bank settings, issue a client link, refund money or deploy payment changes.

## Delivery operating rule

Compress work through parallel cloud research/build/checking, reusable components, structured intake and automated QA. Do not compress required business decisions, client approval, legal/provider review or final testing.

Sohan's desired role is:

- set direction and quality bar;
- choose among well-reasoned options;
- approve consequential external actions;
- test the final experience; and
- make exceptional judgement calls.

The system should handle repetitive research, drafts, project scaffolding, content organization, responsive implementation, routine integrations, test execution, evidence capture, status tracking, handover preparation and post-launch checks.

## External-action boundary

Unless headquarters provides fresh exact approval for a specific action, workers must not:

- send or follow up on outreach;
- publish posts or edit public profiles;
- submit forms, proposals, applications or reviews;
- book calls or meetings;
- deploy public changes;
- activate providers or change account, DNS, KYC, bank or security settings;
- enter passwords, OTPs, keys or payment information;
- expose a private phone, address or credential; or
- spend money.

Workers may research current public evidence, inspect the repository and live site, draft approval-ready materials, create isolated code branches, run focused tests and report exact dependencies.

## Quality gate for every worker output

An output is acceptable only when it:

1. improves qualified demand, conversion, safe payment or reliable delivery;
2. uses current evidence and marks uncertainty;
3. avoids duplicate work and conflicts with this file;
4. names the authoritative source or file used;
5. provides a concrete artifact, tested change or decision—not a generic plan;
6. identifies risks and external dependencies without using them as an excuse for avoidable work;
7. states exactly what was not done; and
8. gives headquarters the smallest next decision required.

## Speed rule

Parallelize independent work. Serialize only shared-state edits and consequential decisions. Use one source of truth, one owner per artifact, reusable templates and one final integration/verification gate.

Twenty agents working on one vague problem are slower than four managers with bounded workers. The target is high-quality compression of controllable work; never fabricate completion because a real-world dependency cannot be accelerated.
