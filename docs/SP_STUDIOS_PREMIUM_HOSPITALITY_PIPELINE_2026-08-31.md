## Response qualification tree

- **No reply**
  - Record only “no verified reply.”
  - Do not follow up, switch channel or advance the next prospect without fresh HQ approval.
- **Decline / already covered**
  - Thank them only if HQ separately approves the exact reply.
  - Record the reason; disqualify or set a dated, evidence-based hold.
- **Permission granted to send the one-page direction**
  - Confirm the responder’s role before treating them as authority.
  - Prepare one tailored page; it is direction, not a free redesign, audit, proposal or speculative build.
  - Obtain HQ approval before sending that second message.
- **Interest without authority**
  - Ask for the single correct owner/routing contact.
  - Do not contact that person until duplicate history and channel are checked and HQ approves.
- **Authority plus credible need**
  - Qualify in order: outcome → scope shape → readiness/content/access → timing → investment fit.
  - One question at a time; do not introduce price before scopeability.
- **Scopeable**
  - Recommend one engagement, one amount, one currency and one realistic delivery window.
  - A proposal is not revenue; progress requires completed SOW/agreement and a verified captured milestone.
- **Red flags**
  - Disqualify or pause for no current trigger, strong owned journey, incumbent without an ownership gap, no operating evidence, no authority, no readiness, insufficient investment fit, or requests outside SP Studios’ complete-website positioning.
