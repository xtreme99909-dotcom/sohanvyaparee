## Official Google facts

1. An eligible business must make in-person contact with customers during stated hours. Online-only brands and businesses are ineligible.  
   Source: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036>

2. A service-area business visits or delivers to customers but does not serve them at its business address. It receives one profile for the overall area. If customers are not served at the base address, the address must be hidden.  
   Source: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481>

3. Up to 20 cities, postcodes or supported areas may be selected. A radius cannot be used, and the overall area should generally remain within roughly two hours' driving time of the operating base.  
   Source: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481>

4. A virtual office is ineligible. A coworking office can be displayed only when SP Studios has clear signage, receives customers there and staffs it with its own people during business hours.  
   Source: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177>

5. Google automatically determines available verification methods and may require more than one. A video-verification recording must be live, unedited, uninterrupted, at least 30 seconds long and uploaded from a mobile device through the profile.  
   Sources: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242> and <https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705>

6. Reviews must reflect genuine experience. Incentives, review gating, selective positive solicitation, pressure, requested wording and conflicted reviews are prohibited.  
   Sources: <https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114> and <https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122>

7. Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence. Service-area entries do not guarantee ranking throughout the selected area, and Google does not sell a better organic local rank.  
   Source: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091>
